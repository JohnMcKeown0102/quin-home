Quin

Quin is a private home assistant that runs on a Raspberry Pi inside one household. It switches the lamps, shows the weather and timers on a wall tablet, and answers simple spoken questions.

Google Calendar

Quin can show the household's upcoming events on the wall tablet. It asks Google only for read-only access to calendar events. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar.

Quin is not a public service and has no other users. There is no account to sign up for.
