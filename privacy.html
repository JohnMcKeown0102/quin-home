Quin privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What Quin accesses

With the account owner's permission, Quin reads calendar events (title, start and end time) from Google Calendar, using the read-only scope calendar.events.readonly. It cannot create, edit or delete events.

How the data is used

Events for the next seven days are shown on a tablet inside the home. They are kept in memory for a few minutes and then fetched again. They are not stored in a database, not written to logs, and not used for anything else.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. Quin runs on a Raspberry Pi on the home network. Calendar data is not sent to any other service, not sold, not shared and not used for advertising or to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account page Third-party apps and services. Quin then stops showing events.
